## Employer & Placement drive records

Institutions must document companies and employers that visit for recruitment, and the placement drives conducted.

Build an employer and placement record management system that avoids duplicating company data across drives.

## Data to be captured:

Company,
Industry/sector;
Company location;
Academic year;
Eligibility criteria;
Drive status;

## UI flow
Companies -> Company profile -> Placement Drives -> Parrticipation

## Minimum expected output
Companies by year
Drives by year
Industry-wise participation
Repeat recruiters
